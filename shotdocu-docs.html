✨ Features

  • One-tap to PDF. Convert JPGs, photos, and other images to high-quality PDFs instantly. No setup, no learning
  • Clean mobile scans. Capture documents, receipts, or notes and get crisp, professional-looking PDFs
  • Combine & organize fast. Merge multiple images, reorder pages, and create one tidy file ready to send
  • Right-size every file. Choose quality level to balance clarity and file size — perfect for email and cloud uploads
  • Always with you. iCloud sync keeps documents updated across your Apple devices
  • Share in a tap. Send via email, iMessage, AirDrop, or save to your favorite cloud
  • Try It for Free. Enjoy a free trial with a limited number of scans — then upgrade for unlimited access
Simplify your document workflow — ShotDocu is here to help.
